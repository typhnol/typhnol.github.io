so i think it should be obvious between this post and the last one that i've either forgotten this blog existed, got stuck in a trench, or died. thankfully, it was not the third one. i still manage to have a sliver of a will left to live.

## my education [jazz hands]

i've been busy suffering through highschool for the past few years in a program that shall not be named, but it's over now and i'm starting college. yay!

so far it's been smooth sailing, but i don't have any doubts it'll pick up soon.

highschool was genuinely so depressing and stress inducing that i only had teeny tiny fragments of free time to myself and creating things

i mean i wasn't a snail at making art by any means, but it was definitely hindered a lot. i'd have posted way more shit during the peak of my undertale hyperfixation if it weren't for me doing homework from 6pm to 12am every day.

regardless, i survived so suck it highschool

![graduation](https://file.garden/Zlk30agh4hF11eOU/blogimages/9-15-26/graduation.png)

## artfight

this was my first year participating in artfight, so that's super fun

it was genuinely really enjoyable actually, more than i thought it would be. given all the negativity surrounding the event on social media, i was almost scared out of joining. not to mention how easily i can get burnt out maintaining projects over longer periods of time.

i joined despite that, and it proved to be a worthwhile choice. i mean, i've NEVER in my ENTIRE life made 14 whole pieces all within a month. that genuinely would've sounded ludicrous to 2023-2024 me when i was making one piece every TWO months. 

it also gave me the chance to experiment and improve my art, so that's always good.

![locked in](https://file.garden/Zlk30agh4hF11eOU/blogimages/9-15-26/artist.png)

## beepbox project???

yeah i'm not gonna sugarcoat it i've have basically zero motivation to pick up this project again. i'll try to, if not for me then for the fact that undertale's 11th anniversary is coming up soon. but no guarantees.

i started the project last year as a sort of homage to undertale since it was undertale's 10th anniversary. but it was mostly on a whim. don't ask me why i tried to recreate the entire soundtrack in beepbox, i couldn't tell you.

however, i do still really want to at least finish this project. maybe if i lock in i can finish the year with a bang.

![broken record](https://file.garden/Zlk30agh4hF11eOU/blogimages/9-15-26/record.png)

## the fate of this blog

i likely won't be posting actively on here since the only things i have to yap about are either my life (which of course i'd rather not divulge too much of) or my interests.

i generally like to draw things out more than i like to write them though, so expect more visual art from me than written yap sessions about my interests. 

i did originally plan to talk about more of the things i like, such as undertale and deltarune, as well as other games i've played and really enjoyed like nine sols or hollow knight and silksong

but alas, too much time has passed since i've last played some of these games, or even interacted with fan media about them, so i don't think i could write up a post about them now even if i wanted to

maybe nine sols will get a revival out of me, since i've very recently started a replay of the game!

anyways, basically this blog is going to be perpetually under life support and i'll probably only have the motivation to breathe life into it every few months.

thanks for reading
